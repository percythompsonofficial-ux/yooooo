# Home service prospect list

111 home service businesses across the Southeast that appear to have **no real website** —
prospects for a web design pitch.

## Read this before using the list

**There are no phone numbers in this file, on purpose.**

An earlier version of this list carried phone numbers. They did not work. The numbers were
never read off a real page — every directory site that would carry them (Yelp, YellowPages,
BBB, Facebook, Nextdoor, Manta, Chamber of Commerce) is blocked by this environment's egress
policy, so the numbers came from search-result summaries instead. Search summaries routinely
pull contact details from similarly-named businesses in other states, and that is the most
likely explanation for the failures. Reverse-lookups on a sample of four returned no matching
business for any of them.

The **names and no-website status** rest on better evidence: the actual URL lists returned by
search. A business was recorded here only when a search for its name and city returned social
and directory links (facebook.com, yelp.com, nextdoor.com, bbb.org) with no own-domain result
among them. That is directly observed, not summarized.

So: treat this as a prospect list, not a call list. Each name is one Google Maps lookup away
from a number you can trust — and that lookup also confirms the business is still active,
which this list does not check.

## Columns

- `web_status`
  - `none` — no website found
  - `broken` — has a link that is dead or points at a discontinued platform
  - `free subdomain` — Wix / Strikingly / Square / Jobber / simplesite subdomain, no real domain
- `notes` — owner names, tenure, service breadth, and anything needing a second look

## Where the value is

The `broken` and `free subdomain` rows are the warmest leads, not the `none` rows. Those
businesses already decided they want a web presence and are living with something dead or
branded for someone else. Five Flags Pressure Washing's domain currently serves
"Site Unavailable." The four Google `business.site` entries are worse — Google discontinued
that product, so their listings point at nothing and the owners may not know.

## Method notes for anyone re-running this

Yield varied far more by **market** than by trade:

- Produced well: Valdosta, Vicksburg, Fayetteville/Hope Mills, Pine Bluff, El Dorado, Statesboro,
  Milledgeville, Anderson, Hammond/Slidell, Columbus/Starkville, Picayune, Waycross
- Produced nothing: Killeen, Huntsville, Chattanooga, Little Rock, Savannah, Pensacola, Columbus GA
- Dead vein by trade: junk removal (0 for 5), pressure washing in mid-size metros

Markets with military bases, tech corridors or tourism money have already been sold websites.
Small and mid-size Deep South markets have not. The tier below that — towns under ~15k — has the
highest no-website rate but the worst directory coverage, so contact details are hardest to get.
