# Photos — drop your real job pictures here

The website looks for the six files listed below. **Until a file exists, that
spot automatically shows a drawn illustration instead** — so the site never
looks broken, and it never shows a stock photo of a yard that isn't yours.

## How to add your photos

1. Pick your best photo for each category (from your phone, camera, or your
   Facebook page).
2. Rename it to the exact filename below (all lowercase, keep the `.jpg`).
3. Drop it into this `public/photos/` folder.
4. That's it — it appears on the site automatically.

**Best results:** landscape (wide) orientation, at least 1200px wide, showing
finished, cleaned-up work in good daylight.

## The six filenames

| Filename                       | Where it shows            |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| `hero.jpg`                     | Big hero + page banners   |
| `landscape-installations.jpg`  | Home + Our Work           |
| `lawn-maintenance.jpg`         | Home + Our Work           |
| `sod-resodding.jpg`            | Home + Our Work           |
| `flower-beds.jpg`              | Our Work                  |
| `shrub-hedge.jpg`              | Our Work                  |
| `tree-trimming.jpg`            | Our Work                  |

`.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, and `.webp` all work — the site checks for each, so
just match the name before the dot (e.g. `lawn-maintenance.png` is fine).
